# Why Don't They Have Any Patience?

About seventy minutes on your own device. Your run carries over to the next class day if you need it.

## The situation

Wexford Benefit Administrators processes dental claims and is replacing its claims system. The vendor needs an accurate account of how claims arrive and reach the first review.

## Your role

You are the process analyst. You watch the review desk at work, then interview three employees, Ray, Terry and Ruth, in fifteen-minute appointments.

## What you'll do

Choose the order of your interviews and ask your own questions; every question takes time from the appointment. Then complete the process chart and write your report.

## What you hand in

A completed process chart and a four-step report the vendor will configure against, then two short review answers. Your instructor leads the class debrief afterwards.
