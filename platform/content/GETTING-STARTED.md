# Getting started with Flexee RapidSims

RapidSims are short decision simulations for business classes. Each one puts students in a real situation with incomplete information, and the debrief turns what they did into the lesson. The catalogue has twelve: ten RapidSims of about twenty minutes plus a debrief, and two RapidSim+ of about an hour.

## 1. Sign in

Open the invitation link in your email and set your password. The link works for 14 days. After that, sign in at rapidsims.flexee.org.

## 2. Create a class

On Your classes, enter a class title and a term, then choose Create class. A class is your whole course. Each time you play a simulation with it, you start a session.

## 3. Add the simulations you want

On Class home, choose Add a simulation. Each one you add appears in the left menu with four pages: Sessions, Past sessions, Rehearse and Play it yourself. About this sim, on the Sessions and Rehearse pages, gives you its one-pager: the situation, why it's hard, what students take away and how to run it.

## 4. Rehearse before you teach

Rehearse plays the simulation with practice students while you watch it from the faculty view, through Results and the debrief. Practice students stay out of your roster and your results. Play it yourself opens the student screens in a new tab, so you see exactly what students see.

## 5. Bring your students in

Class home shows one class link. Send it to your students once; it covers every simulation in the class. Students who open it appear under Students. Choose Give access for each student once their registration is settled.

## 6. Run a session

On the simulation's Sessions page, choose Start session. Students open the simulation from their own page and join the session by themselves. Follow them on the Students and Results tabs, then run the debrief from your screen. Present fills the screen for the projector.

## 7. Ask Claude

Every faculty page has Ask Claude in the lower right corner. Ask how to run a simulation, what a screen means, why a student got a result, or which simulation suits a unit. Answers come from Claude, Anthropic's AI. Claude sees your students as Student 1, Student 2 and so on, and you see their names.

## 8. Light or dark

The sun and moon button switches the student screens and the debrief slides between light and dark. Light often projects better in a bright classroom.
