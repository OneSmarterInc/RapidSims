How the stop condition works

The runner starts at 1 request/second and increases every 15 seconds until it reaches the selected peak. It automatically enters cooldown after two consecutive one-second windows with over 20% errors, a timeout, or p95 latency above 3 seconds. Requests then ramp down to zero.
