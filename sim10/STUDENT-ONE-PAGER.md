# Infrastructure or Bubble?

A reading and a call, then what happened and a class debrief. On your own or with a team.

## The situation

A client wants a straight answer about one company that builds long-lived physical assets. You get two quarters of its real financial statements, with its name removed.

## Your role

You are the client's adviser. If you play in a team, each member makes a call first, then the team writes one answer.

## What you'll do

Read the statements, then make the call: genuine infrastructure or a bubble. Name the line that decided it and the evidence that would change your mind.

## What you hand in

Your call, the line you cited and what would change your mind. Your instructor then shows what happened and leads the class debrief.
