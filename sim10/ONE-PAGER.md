# RapidSim 10 · Infrastructure or Bubble?

A reading and a call, a staged reveal and the debrief. Individual or team.

## The situation

Your students are advisers, and a client wants a straight answer about one company. They get two quarters of its real financial statements with the name removed. It builds long-lived physical assets, and its competitors are building in parallel. Students decide whether it's laying down genuine infrastructure or riding a bubble.

## Why it's hard

The statements show revenue two ways, the company's headline figures and the standard accounting figures, side by side. Both are real, and they tell different stories. The call is binary: students cite the line that decided it and say what evidence would change their mind.

## What students take away

Students learn to read past the headline number to what the business earns and spends, and to commit to a call while naming its weak point. When the reveal arrives, the line they cited matters more than whether the call was right.

## Running it

Before Start you choose one company or two (two make an instructive pair), and you can give students longer to read. In team play each member makes a private call, then the team writes one answer. You move each part along from the session bar, and the Live screen shows the class's calls. The debrief puts opposite calls on the same line side by side. It fits a unit on IT investment cycles, financial analysis or the economics of infrastructure. How would your students tell the difference in a company they're reading about this week?
