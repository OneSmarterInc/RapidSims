# Do We Switch?

A ten-minute decision, then a class debrief. On your own or with a team.

## The situation

A regional retailer's card payments are down, and every minute costs sales at the registers. The company has a backup arrangement for days like this.

## Your role

You make the call: switch to the backup now, or hold where you are. If you play in a team, the first member to decide decides for the team.

## What you'll do

Read the briefing pack while the ten-minute clock runs, then commit to one decision with a one-line reason.

## What you hand in

Your decision and its reason, and two short review answers at the end. Your instructor leads the class debrief afterwards.
