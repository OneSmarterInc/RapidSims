# Midland Equipment

About twenty minutes of play, then a class debrief. On your own or with a team.

## The situation

Midland Equipment is a mid-sized heating and air-conditioning company. You have nine million dollars a year to spread across five technology budget lines.

## Your role

You run Midland's technology. If you play in a team, one member enters the team's decisions and everyone writes their own review.

## What you'll do

Read the briefing and meet the people who want the money. Write a one-line view of what the budget is for, then set the budget for Year 1 and Year 2 and see what each year brings.

## What you hand in

Your two budgets, your opening view and a short review. Your instructor leads the class debrief afterwards.
