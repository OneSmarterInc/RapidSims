# RapidSim 03 · Midland Equipment

About an hour in all: twenty minutes of play and about forty minutes of debrief. Individual or team, chosen when you start the session.

## The situation

Midland Equipment is a mid-sized HVAC company, and your students run its technology. They have nine million dollars a year to spread across five budget lines over two years. What they buy in year one arrives in year two, and a third year runs entirely on what they bought in both.

## Why it's hard

Year one locks in before any result is visible. Four of the five lines carry a yearly cap, and the closing lesson shows what that cap stands for. Every line has a real case behind it, and the numbers are tuned so each allocation buys one thing at the cost of another. Students who try to cover everything find out quickly that the budget asks for a choice.

## What students take away

Infrastructure has a lead time. The decision that matters is made years before the moment that reveals it. Students leave understanding why technology leaders sound cautious about spending that looks optional this year, and they see their own priorities in the numbers.

## Running it

In team play you split the class into teams and each team chooses a lead who enters its decisions. Results show every allocation year by year, and the debrief lets you put two or three side by side and ask what each gave up. Rehearse uses three practice students on one team. It fits a week on IT infrastructure or architecture, or budgeting in a management course. What would your students cut first, and would they still cut it knowing about year three?
