# What Did It Tell Them?

About twenty minutes, on your own device.

## The situation

Calder Sealing Systems makes industrial seals, and its customers ask a support assistant called Relay for specifications. This morning someone noticed that Relay gave a customer a figure from an old datasheet six weeks ago.

## Your role

You are the VP of Customer Operations, and the people involved are on the call with you.

## What you'll do

Question the people on the call directly while the clock runs, and record your reading of the situation at set points. What you do early shapes what happens next.

## What you hand in

Your recorded readings and two short review answers at the end. Your instructor leads the class debrief afterwards.
