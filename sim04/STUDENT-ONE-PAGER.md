# Whose Number Is Right?

About twenty-five minutes. On your own or with a team.

## The situation

Ridgeway Dispatch sells scheduling and invoicing software to trade businesses. The board wants one figure: customer retention for the year.

## Your role

You prepare that figure for the board. If you play in a team, you work it out together.

## What you'll do

You get a roster of forty customer accounts and a reporting guide. Work out the retention figure, commit to it with how confident you are, then see every number in the class side by side.

## What you hand in

One retention figure with your confidence, and short answers to the review questions your instructor opens.
