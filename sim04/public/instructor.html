How a session runs
  1. Create the room and share the class link. Students join with their email.
  2. Choose how many people per group and press Divide randomly. Drag anyone to adjust.
  3. Press Start. Groups get the clock you set to work out one retention number from the same data, using a definition sheet, and lock it.
  4. Show the numbers on the projector, ask two groups to explain theirs, then show the definitions.
  5. Use the private check on your own device to see who is in each group and whether any number was miscalculated.
