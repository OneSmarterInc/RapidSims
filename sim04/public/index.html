Before the clock starts

What happens today

Ridgeway Dispatch sells software that plumbers, heating engineers and landscapers use to schedule jobs and send invoices. Its customers pay every month. The quarter from 1 July to 30 September has just ended. The board chair, Elena Varga, has asked one question: what was our customer retention this quarter? Retention measures how much of its business the company kept. Your group will give her one number.

  1. Your instructor puts you in a group, then starts a 25-minute clock.
  2. When the clock starts, this page shows the records for Ridgeway’s customer accounts, their support tickets, and a definition sheet. The definition sheet says exactly how to count retention for this report.
  3. Use the definition sheet and the account records to work out one percentage. A calculator or spreadsheet helps, and you can download the records as a file.
  4. Lock your number and say how sure you are, from 1 (a guess) to 5 (certain). A locked number cannot be changed. If the clock runs out first, your group reports no number.
  5. When every group has locked a number, your instructor puts the numbers on the main screen and the class talks about them.

In a team, anyone in your group can lock the number for everyone, so agree before anyone presses Lock.
