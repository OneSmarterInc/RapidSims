Facilitator notes

Running the reveal

After Stage 1, ask two groups with far-apart figures to defend their numbers before you reveal the definitions. At Stage 2, name what they just argued about: a number is a definition plus a measurement plus a purpose. Close by asking each student to write down a number they are judged by, its definition, and what that definition leaves out.
