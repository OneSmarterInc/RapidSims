# Eighteen Months Later

A timed round, then what happened next and a class debrief. Best played with a team.

## The situation

A veterinary practice with several clinics needs a new scheduling system. Three vendors are on the shortlist.

## Your role

You choose the system. If you play in a team, your team shares its questions and votes privately on the vendor.

## What you'll do

Put six questions to the three vendors' representatives before the clock runs out; every question goes to all three. Then pick a vendor and a go-live plan, and see how the clinics are doing eighteen months later.

## What you hand in

Your six questions, your vendor and your plan, and two short review answers. Your instructor leads the class debrief afterwards.
