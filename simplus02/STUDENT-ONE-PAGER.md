# Who Pays for the Wire?

About sixty-five minutes, in teams of four, face to face.

## The situation

New data centres want electricity, and a utility has begun a $1.2 billion transmission build to serve them. Someone has to pay for the line, whether or not the demand arrives.

## Your role

Your team of four takes the four seats in a regulatory proceeding: the utility, a data-centre developer, industrial energy users and a consumer advocate. You hold one seat, with its own private brief.

## What you'll do

Read the walkthrough and your brief, give a short opening statement, then negotiate the terms out loud with your team. A term counts once all four seats sign it on the shared term sheet before the deadline.

## What you hand in

Your team's signed term sheet and a short review. Your instructor leads the class debrief afterwards.
