# Where Does the Money Land?

A statement, an allocation, then the years that follow and a class debrief. On your own or with a team.

## The situation

It's June 2000. Analysts are forecasting enormous growth in business-to-business trading over the internet, and five companies are open to investment, each with its real financials.

## Your role

You manage a fund and decide where its money goes.

## What you'll do

First write where you think the value will land and why; that statement locks. Then put the fund's money into the companies or hold it as cash. Your instructor then shows what happened in 2002, 2004 and 2010.

## What you hand in

Your statement and your fund, and a short review. Your instructor leads the class debrief afterwards.
