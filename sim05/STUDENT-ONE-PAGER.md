# Would You Approve This?

Five timed rounds, then a class debrief. On your own or with a team.

## The situation

Loopwell is a fitness app. Its team keeps coming to you with new ways to use customer data, one feature at a time.

## Your role

You are Loopwell's product manager. If you play in a team, each member votes privately and the majority decides.

## What you'll do

A short walkthrough shows how the rounds work. Then, in each of five rounds, you read the request and approve or decline it before the round closes. Decide in time: a round that closes without a decision ships the feature.

## What you hand in

Your five decisions and a short review at the end. Your instructor leads the class debrief afterwards.
