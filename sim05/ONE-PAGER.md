# RapidSim 05 · Would You Approve This?

Five timed rounds plus the debrief. Individual or team.

## The situation

Loopwell is a fitness app, and your students are its product manager. Over five rounds the team brings them a new way to use customer data, one feature at a time. Each request is reasonable, each has a business case, and each touches one customer, Dana Okafor. Students approve or decline as the clock moves.

## Why it's hard

Each request looks sound on its own, and that's the point. Declining has a visible cost every round, a line naming what Dana and the business lose. If a round runs out before students decide, the feature ships, as it usually does in a real company. Students judge each step on its own while the data underneath keeps adding up.

## What students take away

Privacy harm often comes from a run of sensible decisions, each approved by someone who saw only their own piece. Students see what the combined data lets the company know about Dana, from Dana's side of the screen, and the debrief connects that to reviewing data use as a whole.

## Running it

An untimed walkthrough shows students how the rounds work before the clock starts. You open each round from the session bar and close it when the class is done. In team play every member votes privately and the majority decides. Results show each student's ending, and the debrief covers the endings, the round that split the class and what shipped. It fits a unit on data ethics, privacy or product management. Where in your students' future jobs will they be the one approving round three?
