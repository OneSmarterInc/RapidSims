# Disaster or Breach?

About twenty minutes, on your own device.

## The situation

It's two in the morning at Northbeam Data Services, which runs the data platforms behind about forty companies. Several applications are throwing corruption errors, and the incident call is already open.

## Your role

You are the VP of Operations. Four people are on the call: the infrastructure director, the head of security, the storage vendor and the client lead. Each knows part of the picture.

## What you'll do

Ask anyone on the call anything, in your own words, and take people aside one to one when you want to. At three points in the night you record what you think is happening and what you'll do about it.

## What you hand in

Your three recorded positions, each with what would change your mind, and two short review answers at the end. Your instructor leads the class debrief afterwards.
