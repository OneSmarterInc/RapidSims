# Would You Have Bought It?

A ten-minute decision, then the years that follow and a class debrief. On your own or with a team.

## The situation

It's autumn 2000, and you run the largest video rental chain in the country. A small company that rents DVDs by mail has offered itself to you for fifty million dollars.

## Your role

You are the chief executive. If you play in a team, each member votes privately and the majority decides.

## What you'll do

Read the briefing and hear from four advisers. Write a one-sentence reason, then decide to buy or decline before the clock runs out. Your instructor then shows what happened in 2002, 2005 and 2010.

## What you hand in

Your decision and its reason, which stays with you through every year that follows, and two short review answers. Your instructor leads the class debrief.
